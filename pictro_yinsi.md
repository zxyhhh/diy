# Pictro Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 14, 2026**  
**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

The developer of Pictro (referred to as “Pictro,” “we,” “us,” or “our”; the developer identity is the one shown on the applicable app-store product page) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Pictro handles and protects information and describes your choices and rights.

This Policy applies to the Pictro mobile application. By using Pictro, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app.

## 1. Privacy at a Glance

- Pictro does not require an account.
- Your photos, videos, camera feeds, recordings, and editing content are processed on your device by default and are not uploaded to servers operated by Pictro.
- Pictro does not sell personal information, display third-party advertising, or use collected information for cross-app tracking.
- The iOS version uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect limited usage and device information so that we can understand feature usage and improve the product.
- Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store. Pictro does not receive or store payment-card credentials.

## 2. Information We Handle

### 2.1 Photos, Videos, Camera, and Microphone Content

When you choose to use photography, video recording, dual-camera recording, mosaic, poster creation, batch watermarking, video conversion, saving, or sharing features, Pictro may access or process on your device:

- live camera feeds and photos you capture;
- audio recorded by the microphone;
- photos or videos you select from the system photo library;
- text, stickers, watermarks, posters, GIFs, Live Photos, and other edited results you create; and
- temporary files created to edit, preview, combine, save, or share content.

This content is processed on your device by default. Pictro does not upload it to servers operated by Pictro. Temporary files may remain in the app sandbox or operating-system temporary storage until removed by the app or operating system. Content that you save remains in the system photo library until you delete it.

Photos, videos, and recordings may contain faces, voices, location clues, or other sensitive information. Pictro does not intentionally identify or extract that information or collect it on Pictro-operated servers. Only use content you are entitled to process, and obtain any required permission before recording or sharing information about others.

### 2.2 Usage and Analytics Data

To understand feature usage, identify issues, and improve Pictro, the iOS version uses Google Analytics for Firebase. The service may automatically process:

- product interactions such as app launches, sessions, and screen views;
- technical details such as app version, device type or model, operating-system version, language, and time zone;
- a randomly generated app-instance identifier and device-level identifiers provided by the platform; and
- non-precise location information such as approximate region. Approximate region may be inferred from network information; Pictro does not request or read precise device-location permission.

Pictro does not send your photos, videos, recordings, posters, text, or other creative content to Firebase. We do not use Analytics data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Google processes this data as our analytics provider under its terms and privacy policies. Retention of user-level and event-level Google Analytics data depends on the Analytics property settings. Standard properties generally offer retention settings of 2 or 14 months, while aggregated statistical reports may be retained for longer.

### 2.3 Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase or restore Pictro Pro, the Apple App Store and StoreKit process the transaction. Pictro may read:

- product identifiers, localized prices, and currency;
- purchase, subscription, restoration, refund, or revocation status; and
- Apple-verified current entitlements and offer eligibility.

We use this information to display prices, complete and restore purchases, verify entitlements, and unlock Pro features. Pictro does not receive or store your Apple ID password, payment-card number, or complete payment details. Apple handles and retains purchase history and payment-related data under its terms, privacy policies, and legal obligations.

### 2.4 Feedback and Contact Information

When you tap “Feedback,” Pictro only attempts to open an external email app on your device. We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include only after you send the email. We use this information to answer questions, process feedback, and provide support.

Feedback emails are processed by your chosen email provider and ours. We do not use feedback content for unrelated purposes unless necessary to resolve an issue, comply with law, or handle a dispute.

### 2.5 Sharing with Other Apps

When you choose “Share,” Pictro opens the system share sheet and lets you choose the third-party app or person that will receive the photo, video, or other file. After sharing, the recipient processes that content under its own privacy policy. Review the recipient and sharing scope before proceeding.

## 3. Device Permissions

Pictro may request these permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Effect if Denied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Capture photos and videos and provide dual-camera features | Capture-related features will not work |
| Microphone | Record sound while recording video | Videos may have no audio, or related recording features may be unavailable |
| Photo-library read access | Select photos or videos for editing and conversion | You cannot import photos or videos |
| Photo-library add access | Save generated photos, videos, GIFs, or Live Photos | Pictro cannot save directly to the system photo library |

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Withdrawing a permission does not affect processing already completed under valid authorization, but related features may stop working.

Pictro does not request access to contacts, health data, Bluetooth, or precise location. Any place name or coordinates shown in the watermark interface are sample content and do not mean that Pictro obtained your actual location.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

- **On-device content:** Temporary files are removed by the app or operating system. You control content saved to the photo library. Uninstalling Pictro normally removes app-sandbox data, but not content already saved to the photo library or shared with third parties.
- **Analytics data:** Google retains this data according to the applicable Google Analytics settings and policies. Event-level data is deleted under Google’s process after the applicable period; aggregated statistics may be retained for longer.
- **Purchase information:** Apple retains purchase records under its policies and financial, tax, and other legal obligations. Uninstalling Pictro does not delete App Store purchase history.
- **Feedback email:** We normally retain it until the issue is resolved and may keep it longer when necessary for disputes, security incidents, or legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion.

We retain information under our control only for as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements.
