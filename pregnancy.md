# Pregnancy Test 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

## 中文

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

本政策说明 Pregnancy Test（怀孕测试，以下简称“本应用”）如何处理您的信息。

### 1. 我们处理的信息与用途

- **您选择的照片及识别结果**：当您主动提交分析时，您选择或拍摄并编辑的试纸照片会通过网络发送至识别服务及相关服务提供方，用于生成结果说明。照片和结果可能包含敏感的健康信息。请仅提交您有权使用的照片，避免包含人脸、姓名或其他无关个人信息。
- **本地记录与设置**：您选择保存的照片、分析结果、记录时间，以及语言选择和首次使用设置，保存在设备上，用于历史查看和应用设置。应用不提供历史记录的云同步功能。
- **购买与订阅信息**：应用商店与 RevenueCat 处理匿名应用用户标识、商品标识、购买凭证和订阅状态，用于验证购买、恢复会员及提供会员权益。
- **广告相关信息**：Google AdMob 及其服务提供方可能处理设备标识、网络地址、广告展示与互动数据及诊断信息，用于投放广告、防范作弊和衡量效果。我们不会将试纸照片、分析结果或健康记录提供给广告服务。激励广告仅在收到完成奖励后增加分析额度。
- **您主动联系时提供的信息**：如果您通过邮件联系我们，我们会处理您的邮箱地址和邮件内容，用于回应您的问题。

本应用无需注册账号，不要求您提供姓名、电话号码或住址。本应用提供可选订阅和广告。订阅通过应用商店处理，我们无法获取您的完整支付卡信息。

### 2. 相机与照片访问

拍照或选择照片时，本应用会使用系统提供的相机和照片选择功能，并在系统要求时请求相关权限。我们只处理您主动选择用于本应用的照片，不会自动上传整个相册。您可以通过设备系统设置管理权限；拒绝权限可能影响对应功能。

### 3. 在线处理与服务提供方

分析照片需要联网，识别并非完全在设备本地完成。我们会将完成分析所必需的照片和语言选项提供给相关服务提供方；这些提供方也可能处理网络连接和服务运行信息。

本应用自身不建立在线照片或分析历史数据库。相关服务提供方可能为完成处理、保障安全或满足法律要求保留请求内容及运行记录，其处理和保留方式取决于适用的服务安排及隐私规则。删除本地记录不会自动删除服务提供方可能保留的信息。

我们不出售您的个人信息，也不将试纸照片或分析结果用于广告定向。除提供本应用服务、遵守法律义务或保护合法权益所必要的情形外，我们不会披露您的个人信息。在线处理可能涉及您所在国家或地区以外的服务设施。

订阅与广告服务的具体处理方式可参阅 [RevenueCat 隐私政策](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) 和 [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

### 4. 保存与删除

您保存的本地记录会保留至您删除为止。您可以在历史记录中删除单条记录，或在设置中清空历史记录，删除本应用保存的相应照片与结果；这不会删除您原本保存在系统相册中的照片。卸载应用通常会移除设备上的应用数据，设备或系统备份中的副本由相应备份设置管理。

必要的运行记录仅在提供服务、安全维护或履行法律义务所需的期间内保留。您可以通过下方邮箱提出隐私问题或数据处理请求。对于已经匿名化、无法关联到您的信息，我们可能无法定位特定记录。

### 5. 信息安全与您的选择

在线分析使用加密连接传输数据。我们采取合理措施保护信息，但任何传输或存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。您可在应用商店中管理或取消订阅，并在应用设置中使用适用的广告隐私选项。取消订阅不会删除记录，删除记录不会取消订阅。您可以不提交照片、撤回系统权限、删除本地记录或停止使用本应用；不提交照片时，在线分析功能无法使用。

根据您所在地区适用的法律，您可能享有访问、更正、删除个人信息、限制或反对处理，以及撤回同意等权利。撤回同意不影响撤回之前已进行处理的合法性。您可通过下方邮箱联系我们。

### 6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。若您认为儿童在未经适当授权的情况下向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，以便核实和处理。

### 7. 政策更新

本政策可能随功能或数据处理方式变化而更新。更新后的政策会标明新的生效日期；重大变化将通过适当方式告知。

## English

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how Pregnancy Test (the “App”) handles your information.

### 1. Information we process and why

- **Selected photos and analysis results:** When you submit a photo for analysis, the pregnancy test photo you select or capture and edit is sent over the internet to the recognition service and its service providers to generate a result explanation. Photos and results may contain sensitive health information. Only submit photos you are authorized to use, and avoid including faces, names, or unrelated personal information.
- **Local records and settings:** Photos and results you choose to save, record timestamps, your language choice, and onboarding settings are stored on your device for history and preferences. The App does not offer cloud synchronization of your history.
- **Purchases and subscriptions:** The app store and RevenueCat process an anonymous app user identifier, product identifiers, purchase receipts, and subscription status to validate purchases, restore membership, and provide benefits.
- **Advertising information:** Google AdMob and its service providers may process device identifiers, network addresses, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. We do not provide pregnancy test photos, analysis results, or health records to advertising services. Rewarded ads add analysis credit only after a completion reward is received.
- **Information you provide when contacting us:** If you email us, we process your email address and message to respond to your inquiry.

The App does not require an account or ask for your name, telephone number, or home address. The App offers optional subscriptions and advertising. Purchases are processed by the app store; we do not receive your full payment card details.

### 2. Camera and photo access

The App uses system camera and photo selection features and requests permissions where required by your device. We process only the photos you actively select for use in the App; we do not automatically upload your entire photo library. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may affect the corresponding feature.

### 3. Online processing and service providers

Photo analysis requires an internet connection and is not performed entirely on your device. We provide the photo and language preference necessary for analysis to the relevant service providers. These providers may also process connection and service operation information.

The App itself does not maintain an online photo or analysis history database. Service providers may retain request content and operational records to complete processing, maintain security, or meet legal requirements. Their processing and retention depend on the applicable service arrangements and privacy rules. Deleting local history does not automatically delete information that service providers may retain.

We do not sell your personal information or use pregnancy test photos or analysis results for advertising targeting. We do not disclose personal information except as necessary to provide the App’s services, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate rights. Online processing may involve facilities outside your country or region.

For details about subscription and advertising processing, see the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Retention and deletion

Saved local records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual records in your history or clear all history in settings to remove the corresponding photos and results saved by the App. This does not delete original photos from your device’s photo library. Uninstalling the App generally removes its on-device data; copies in device or system backups are managed through the relevant backup settings.

Essential operational records are retained only for the period needed to provide the service, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. You may contact the email address below with privacy questions or data requests. We may be unable to locate specific records that have been anonymized and cannot be linked to you.

### 5. Security and your choices

Online analysis uses encrypted connections to transmit data. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your app store and use applicable ad privacy choices in the App settings. Canceling a subscription does not delete history, and deleting history does not cancel a subscription. You may choose not to submit photos, revoke system permissions, delete local records, or stop using the App. Online analysis is unavailable without submitting a photo.

Depending on the laws applicable where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Contact us using the email address below to make a request.

### 6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so we can investigate and address the matter.

### 7. Policy updates

We may update this policy as features or data practices change. Updated policies will show a new effective date, and material changes will be communicated through appropriate means.
