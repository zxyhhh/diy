# Car Stop Privacy Policy

**Developer**　fuxing_app  
**Effective date**　November 8, 2026  
**Contact**　zxyhdream@gmail.com

This policy applies to Car Stop across all platforms and distribution channels. It explains how we handle information related to gameplay, advertising, purchases, and support, and how you can manage that information. The availability of ads, purchases, and related privacy options varies by platform, region, and version.

## 1. Game data

Your game progress, settings, and any profile details you choose to enter are stored on your device. We use them to let you resume play, display your profile, and remember your preferences. The game also reads your device language setting to display the appropriate interface language.

## 2. Ads and purchases

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, third-party advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to display and measure ads, troubleshoot issues, and prevent fraud. You can manage available ad privacy options in your device settings.

When you make or restore a purchase, third-party payment and purchase services process transaction records and related identifiers to handle payments, verify transactions, and grant or restore eligible benefits. Purchase restoration is separate from local game saves. If you clear your local data, restoring purchases cannot recover your level progress or other local game data.

## 3. Support, sharing, and security

If you contact us, we use your contact details, message, and any relevant information you choose to provide to handle your request. For example, we may ask for an order number to look into a purchase issue.

Third-party services process relevant information for the advertising, payment, and purchase verification purposes described above. We may also disclose necessary information when required by applicable law. Advertising and purchase services transmit relevant data over encrypted connections.

## 4. Retention and deletion

You can clear Car Stop’s local data using the app data controls available on your platform. This removes game progress and settings stored on the device. Third-party services handle transaction records and other information they hold under their own policies. Clearing local game data does not delete those records.

We keep records we manage only as long as needed to respond to support requests, verify purchases, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

## 5. Your rights and policy updates

To access, correct, or delete personal information we manage, or to ask about this policy, contact us at the email address above. We may need to verify your request to protect your information. We handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

When the game’s features or data practices change, we will post an updated policy on this page with its effective date.
