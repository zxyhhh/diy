### 1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how habit60 ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you use Habit60 and the choices available to you. It covers the current iOS version. Habit60 provides habit, goal, check-in, journal, personal progress, and optional membership features. No Habit60 account registration is required.

Your core records are stored on your device and are not automatically uploaded or synchronized to a cloud service operated by us. Usage analytics and membership verification involve online processing, as described below.

### 2. Personal records on your device

The app stores information you enter, select, or generate locally to provide its features, including:

- Profile details: your chosen nickname, personal intentions, and profile image.
- Plans and records: habit names, goals, schedules, check-ins, progress, milestones, 60-day challenges, experience points, and related statistics.
- Journals and photos: journal and review entries, and photos you add to habits or goals.
- Settings: language, appearance, reminders, and other preferences.
- Authorized health metrics and related records used for habit progress.

We do not automatically receive the contents of these records or require your government identification number, contacts, or precise location. Please take care when including other people's personal information in text or photos.

### 3. Optional permissions and health data

The app requests permissions when relevant features need them. You can decline or revoke them in iOS settings. This affects the corresponding feature; features that do not depend on that permission remain available.

- **Camera and photos:** To take or select profile, habit, and goal progress photos. Selected images remain on your device and are not automatically uploaded.
- **Notifications:** To schedule local habit reminders. Reminders may show habit names; you can adjust notification previews in system settings.
- **Files and clipboard:** Used when you initiate operations such as exporting, importing, or copying backups, for backup and restoration.
- **Apple Health:** After you choose to link a health metric and authorize access, the app reads the relevant data with read-only permission to calculate habit progress on your device. Supported metrics may include steps, sleep, walking/running and cycling distance, active energy, dietary energy, protein, exercise duration, workout count, standing time, flights climbed, mindfulness time, and water intake. Availability depends on your device, system, and authorization.

Habit60 does not write data to Apple Health, upload health data to our servers, or use it for advertising, marketing, or user profiling. You can revoke access in Apple Health or system settings and continue recording manually. Revoking permission does not automatically delete progress records already stored in the app; those records must be deleted separately.

### 4. Usage analytics

The app uses a third-party analytics service to understand usage and improve the product. The current version enables automatic analytics at startup. Processing may include app-instance identifiers, device and system details, app version, language, and default events such as app opens, sessions, and page visits. Network connections may also involve information such as IP addresses, subject to the service's configuration and privacy rules.

The current version does not intentionally send habit or goal content, journals, photos, or health data as custom analytics content. Analytics identifiers do not mean information is fully anonymous. There is currently no separate in-app analytics opt-out switch. Contact us below with questions or rights requests. Revoking camera, notification, or health permissions does not automatically stop analytics processing.

### 5. Purchases and membership verification

Apple's App Store processes payments. We and third-party membership verification providers process anonymous app user identifiers, product identifiers, transaction and purchase verification information, membership entitlement status, and device and network information needed for the service. This supports purchase verification, restoration, entitlement updates, and prevention of transaction abuse. Membership initialization may establish network connections even before you make a purchase.

Habit60 does not request your Apple account password or full payment-card details, or provide your habits, goals, journals, photos, or health records to membership verification providers. An anonymous membership identifier distinguishes app users; it does not mean all purchase information is incapable of being associated with an individual.

### 6. Sharing, backups, and widgets

We do not sell your personal records. Third-party analytics, payment, and membership verification services process the information described in this policy for their respective functions. We may also disclose information we actually hold where required by law or necessary to lawfully protect legitimate rights.

When you choose to share images or files, export a backup, or copy data to the clipboard, selected content leaves the app's local storage. Recipients, destination apps, or storage services may access and process it. Backups may contain journals, photos, health-related progress, and other personal records. Current exported backups have no app-provided password protection. Keep them secure and share carefully.

Home screen widgets read display information, such as today's completion status and the next habit, through shared app storage on your device. People who can view your screen may see this information after you add a widget. You can remove the widget.

iOS or iCloud system backups may include app data depending on your system settings. Those backups are managed by the system service provider. We cannot control copies you export, share, or store with other services.

### 7. Retention and security

Local records remain on your device until you delete the relevant content, clear local data, or remove the relevant app data through the operating system. Deleting a habit or goal may not remove its history. Use the in-app clear-local-data feature for overall deletion. Exported backups, system backups, and shared copies must be managed separately.

Retention of online analytics and transaction information depends on service configuration, the period necessary to provide services, and applicable fraud prevention, tax, or other legal requirements. Clearing local data does not automatically delete analytics or transaction information already held by third-party services.

We take reasonable protective measures appropriate to the processing, but no device or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device passcode, keep your system updated, and store backups in trusted locations.

### 8. Processing outside your country or region

Local records are stored on your device. Online analytics, payment, and membership verification services may process relevant information outside your country or region. Processing locations for third-party services and system backups depend on their service arrangements. We will meet applicable legal obligations for international processing. This policy does not replace any separate consent or other procedure required by law.

### 9. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit records, export backups, delete relevant content, or use the clear-local-data feature on the profile page to remove local activity records managed by the app. You can also manage permissions in system settings.

Clearing data or uninstalling the app does not cancel subscriptions. Manage subscriptions under your Apple account's Subscriptions settings. Restoring purchases restores membership entitlements only, not local habits, goals, or journals.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain copies of or port information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent regulator. Send requests to the email below. After reasonably verifying your identity, we will handle requests under applicable law. We cannot remotely access or recover records stored only on your device. Withdrawing consent does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal.

### 10. Children and minors

Habit60 is not specifically designed for children. Minors should use it with a parent or guardian's guidance and comply with the app store's age and purchase requirements. Obtain guardian consent where required by applicable law. Please do not include unnecessary children's personal information in feedback sent to us. Contact us if you believe a child's information was improperly provided; we will address it under applicable law.

### 11. Policy updates

We may update this policy when features or data practices change and will show the update date. For material changes involving personal information processing, we will provide notice and obtain renewed consent where required by applicable law. Chinese and English versions are available. Neither version limits rights granted by applicable law.
