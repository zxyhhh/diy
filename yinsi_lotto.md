This Privacy Policy explains how LottoKnow (the “app,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

### 1. Information we process

- **Ticket records and preferences:** Saved numbers, games, draw dates, regions, comparison results, language, appearance, and feature usage records are primarily stored on your device.
- **Images you select:** When you scan a ticket, the photo you take or select is uploaded and processed by a recognition service to read ticket information. Avoid uploading images containing unrelated personal information.
- **Lookup information:** Draw lookups and number suggestions use your selected game, date, relevant numbers, and other information needed to complete the feature.
- **Purchase information:** When you use membership services, the app store and subscription service providers process purchase records, subscription status, and identifiers associated with your access to verify purchases and restore benefits. We do not receive your full payment card details.
- **Basic usage information:** Network addresses, basic device and app information, errors, and usage records may be processed to operate the service, maintain security, and resolve problems.
- **Feedback:** Your email address, descriptions, screenshots, and attachments that you voluntarily send are used to handle and respond to your request.

No account registration is required. The app does not obtain your precise location. You choose your default state or region.

### 2. How we use information

We use this information to provide requested features, retain records and preferences, manage membership access, protect the service, and respond to feedback and privacy requests.

We do not sell personal information, use uploaded ticket images for targeted advertising, or proactively use those images to train our own models. Recognition providers handle and retain information under their applicable policies and service arrangements.

### 3. Information sharing

Relevant service providers may process necessary information to provide image recognition, draw lookups, subscription management, and customer support. We share information needed for these purposes. Draw lookups do not require uploading all tickets saved on your device.

We may also disclose information as required by applicable law, valid lawful requests, or where necessary to protect users and service security.

### 4. Permissions and choices

Camera access is used to photograph tickets, photo access to read selected images, and notifications for draw reminders you set. You decide whether to grant permissions and can revoke them in system settings.

Denying a permission may affect its related feature. You can choose manual entry instead of scanning. No image upload is required if you do not use scanning.

### 5. Retention and deletion

You can delete saved tickets in the app. Clearing app data, deleting the app, or changing devices may cause local records to be lost. Whether system backups retain or restore data depends on your device settings.

Uploaded images are used for recognition and are not kept by the app as a cloud ticket history. Service providers may retain information related to processing under their applicable policies and service arrangements. Feedback and necessary service records are retained as needed to handle requests, maintain security, or meet applicable obligations.

Deleting tickets or the app does not cancel subscriptions or automatically delete purchase records that the app store or subscription provider needs to retain. Manage subscriptions through the purchasing app store.

### 6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. No method of information transmission or storage is completely secure.

Services may operate in different countries or regions, and your information may be processed outside your location. We will apply necessary protections in accordance with applicable requirements.

### 7. Your rights and contact

For questions about this policy or requests to access, correct, delete information, or exercise other applicable privacy rights, email [zxyhdream@gmail.com](mailto:zxyhdream@gmail.com). We may carry out necessary verification to protect your information.

Records stored only on your device must be managed on that device. Other requests are handled according to the information we actually hold, applicable requirements, and necessary retention obligations.

### 8. Age requirements

The app is not intended for users below the legal lottery participation age in their location and is not directed at children. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to us, contact the email above.

### 9. Policy updates

When this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice or obtain necessary consent as required.
