This Privacy Policy explains how Aphoto+ handles information when you use photo editing, AI creation and subscription services. Contact the email above with privacy questions or data rights requests.

### 1. On-device editing and permissions

Filters, adjustments and beauty editing take place on your device. Related photos, drafts and creations are stored on your device and are not automatically uploaded through local editing. Beauty tools analyze facial features only to adjust photo effects, not to identify you.

We access selected photos through the system photo picker or photo permissions you grant. Exporting to your photo library may require write permission. If you use a camera entry point, the app requests camera permission to capture a photo you choose to edit. Denying camera access does not prevent selecting an existing photo. You can change permissions in system settings. Refusing access may affect importing or exporting, but does not cancel a subscription. Granting photo access does not cause us to upload your entire library.

### 2. Information processed for AI creation

Only when you submit an AI creation are your selected photo, any text you provide and your creative choices uploaded and processed by us and our AI service partners to complete your creation. Submit only photos you have the right to use, and avoid unnecessary sensitive information such as identity documents or financial records.

We also process creation progress, generated results, usage counts and necessary error information to deliver results, resume interrupted creations and avoid duplicate processing. Uploaded photos for successful creations are removed from our creation storage after the result is saved. Generated results are normally retained for 7 days; uploaded photos for unfinished creations are normally retained for up to 7 days from submission. Necessary usage and security records may be retained longer. These periods apply to creation content held by us; see our partners' privacy policies for their retention practices.

### 3. Subscriptions and purchases

We process an identifier that distinguishes app users, membership status and AI usage records to verify subscription access, restore purchases and calculate available allowances. You do not need to register an account with your name or email to use the app.

Our subscription service partner helps manage membership access and restore purchases. Relevant information includes app user identifiers, purchase and transaction records, purchased products, subscription expiry, membership status and necessary device and app information. Your app store handles payment; we do not directly collect payment card numbers. Our partner and the stores process information under their respective policies.

### 4. Usage statistics and service information

The iOS app uses a statistics service to help us understand app openings, usage frequency and feature use. This may involve app usage identifiers, device and operating system information, app versions, interaction records and approximate region. Collection depends on the platform, permissions and service settings. We do not use photos, creative text or facial analysis results for usage statistics.

To provide online services, maintain security and investigate problems, we and our partners may also process IP addresses, access times and necessary error records. Usage statistics do not cause your entire photo library to be uploaded.

### 5. Purposes and service providers

We use this information to provide editing and AI creation, verify and restore subscriptions, prevent abuse and duplicate usage charges, maintain security, improve reliability and respond to requests. We do not sell your photos or personal information, or publish submitted photos as public creations. Aphoto+ does not itself use submitted content to train models. Third-party model processing, retention and data use are governed by the terms applicable to that service.

We work with Cloudflare for network and cloud services, Google for AI processing and usage statistics, RevenueCat for subscription management, and the relevant app store. Information may be processed outside your country or region. We disclose information where necessary to provide the service, fulfill your request, meet legal obligations or protect lawful interests.


### 6. Retention, security and local deletion

We retain information only as needed to provide services, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Moving a creation to the trash or removing it from the library does not mean that all related photo copies on your device have been deleted. To clear files stored by the app on your device, use the system-provided method for removing app data and back up important creations first. Exported photos and system backups must be managed separately. Uninstalling the app or deleting photos does not cancel a subscription or automatically delete records held by us or our partners.

### 7. Your choices and rights

You may avoid submitting AI tasks and continue using available on-device features, and manage photo permissions in system settings. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email zxyhdream@gmail.com with your request and only the information needed to help us locate the relevant records. Do not send passwords or full payment card details. To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity and the information covered by your request.

Contact us by email to request that usage statistics processing stop or that personal information held by us be deleted. Necessary transaction, legal and abuse-prevention records may be retained as permitted or required by law. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed before withdrawal.

### 8. Children, updates and contact

Minors should use the app with guardian guidance and comply with store age requirements. We do not aim to collect children's personal information. A guardian who believes a child submitted information without appropriate authorization should contact us.

We may update this policy as features or services change and revise the effective date. We will explain material changes to processing purposes or scope through appropriate notice and seek separate authorization where legally required. For privacy questions or requests, contact Aphoto+ at zxyhdream@gmail.com.

### 9. Support emails

When you contact us, we process your email address, message and any device details, screenshots, creation issues or order references you choose to provide to investigate, reply and follow up. Provide only necessary information and hide unrelated personal details in screenshots. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, necessary disputes and legal obligations. You may request deletion through the same email address.
