# Speed Test Privacy Policy

**Last updated**　November 24, 2026  
**Developer**　zxy  
**Privacy contact**　zxyhdream@gmail.com

This policy applies to Speed Test for Android. It explains how the app handles data when you run a test, view network details, or use purchase features. You can use the app without creating a Speed Test account.

## Test history and device permissions

Speed test history, ping test history, and unit preferences are stored on your device so you can review and compare results. Devices found on your local network are shown during the current scan. The app does not upload the device list.

The app requests location permission only when you choose to view your Wi-Fi name. It uses this permission to read the name of the network you are connected to. You can revoke the permission in your device settings.

## Network tests and lookups

When you run a speed test, the app exchanges test data with speed test providers to measure download speed, upload speed, and latency. These providers receive your public IP address. Some speed test connections may be unencrypted and carry the test data used to measure speed.

To show network details, the app uses a network lookup service to look up your approximate region, time zone, or internet provider from your public IP address. The service may retain request records under its own privacy policy. When you run a ping test, the app sends test requests to the website or network address you enter.

## Purchase records

Google Play processes payments and handles your payment information. The app and its purchase verification provider use purchase records and an identifier generated for the app to verify purchases and restore access to purchased features.

## Retention, deletion, and contact

You can delete test history and preferences from your device by clearing the app's data in Android settings. Service providers retain and handle network lookup and purchase-related records under their own privacy policies.
