# Pictro Technical Support

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

Thank you for using Pictro. If you experience an issue while capturing, editing, saving, sharing, or making a purchase, review the guidance below. If the problem continues, contact us by email.

When contacting support, please include:

- your iOS or iPadOS version;
- your Pictro version;

## System Requirements

- Pictro supports iPhone and iPad devices running **iOS 15 or later**.
- Dual-camera features depend on multi-camera hardware and are not available on every device.
- Live Photo creation is available only on supported iPhone devices. Other devices can still use GIF conversion.
- Capture, video composition, GIF conversion, and batch processing require sufficient free device storage.
- For the best compatibility, use the latest available versions of Pictro and the operating system.

## Main Features

- simultaneous front- and rear-camera preview and recording;
- retro CCD and watermark cameras;
- video conversion to GIF or Live Photo;
- poster editing with images, text, stickers, and custom backgrounds;
- mosaic tools for images;
- batch watermarking for multiple photos; and
- saving to the system photo library or sharing through the system share sheet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. The camera does not start or says “Camera unavailable”

Try these steps in order:

1. Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and allow Pictro to use the camera.
2. To record sound, also allow Pictro under Microphone permissions.
3. Close other apps using the camera, such as Camera, video-conferencing, or live-streaming apps.
4. Fully close and reopen Pictro.
5. If the issue continues, restart the device and confirm that iOS is up to date.

### 2. Why is dual-camera recording unavailable?

Dual-camera recording requires a device that can run multiple cameras simultaneously. If Pictro says that the device cannot show the front and rear cameras together, the device or selected camera combination generally does not support the feature.

Close other apps using the camera, restart Pictro, or use a standard camera feature instead. Even on a supported device, available camera combinations, zoom range, and visual results may vary by model.

### 3. I cannot import a photo or video

1. Open iOS Settings > Pictro > Photos.
2. Allow photo access. If you use Selected Photos, make sure the intended photo or video is included.
3. Confirm that the file still exists and has been fully downloaded from iCloud to the device.
4. Try another photo or video to rule out file corruption or an unsupported format.

Pictro reads and processes selected media on your device by default and does not upload your creative content to Pictro-operated servers.

### 4. I cannot save to the photo library

Check that:

- iOS Settings > Pictro > Photos allows Pictro to add photos;
- the device has enough free storage;
- the system photo library or iCloud Photos is currently available; and
- Pictro is up to date.

After changing permission, return to Pictro and save again. Files already sent to another app through the system share sheet are managed by the recipient.

### 5. Recording failed, video composition failed, or the video has no sound

- Confirm that camera and microphone permissions are enabled.
- Check the available storage on the device.
- Avoid switching to another app that heavily uses the camera or system resources while recording.
- If Pictro cannot combine both camera recordings into a picture-in-picture video, it may export the front and rear videos separately.
- Fully close Pictro, restart the device, and try again.

### 6. GIF or Live Photo conversion failed

- Confirm that the source video plays correctly in the system Photos app.
- Try a shorter clip or reduce GIF dimensions and frame rate.
- Make sure the device has sufficient free storage.
- Some damaged, protected, or unsupported video formats cannot be converted.
- Live Photos require a compatible iPhone. Use GIF output on other devices.

### 7. Image, poster, mosaic, or batch-watermark processing failed

- Try smaller images or process fewer images in one batch.
- Confirm that each source image opens normally in the system photo library.
- Leave enough free storage for iOS and Pictro.
- If only one image fails, export it again or try a different file.

### 8. App Store prices are missing or a purchase cannot be completed

Confirm that:

1. the device has an internet connection and the App Store opens normally;
2. you are signed in with an Apple ID that can make purchases;
3. the Apple ID payment method, region, and purchase restrictions are valid;
4. in-app purchases are not blocked by Screen Time; and
5. you have tried again later in case the App Store is temporarily unavailable.

Prices, currencies, trial eligibility, and final charges are determined by Apple’s system purchase sheet. Pictro does not receive your payment-card information.

### 9. I purchased Pictro Pro, but features are still locked

1. Confirm that the device is signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
2. Open Pictro > My > Unlock Features.
3. Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the page.
4. Wait for Apple to verify the purchase, then reopen the relevant feature.
5. If access is not restored, restart Pictro and the device and confirm that the subscription remains active.

Restore Purchases does not charge you again. Lifetime access does not renew; Apple manages the renewal status of monthly and yearly subscriptions.

### 10. How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

On an iPhone or iPad, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Pictro to view, change, or cancel an auto-renewing membership.

After cancellation, access normally continues through the end of the current paid period. Apple determines refund eligibility and processes refunds; contact Apple Support if you need to request one.

### 11. How do I change the language or appearance?

Open Pictro > My:

- tap Language to select a supported interface language; and
- tap Theme to choose Light, Dark, or Follow System.

### 12. How does Pictro protect my photos and videos?

Pictro processes photos, videos, and camera content on your device by default and does not upload this creative content to Pictro-operated servers. Content is written to the photo library or sent to a third-party app only when you choose to save or share it.

For details about Firebase Analytics, Apple purchases, and data handling, read the [Pictro Privacy Policy](./yinsi.md).
