# Thenline Privacy Policy

**Effective date and last updated: August 23, 2026**

Thenline is a local-first private timeline journal. This Privacy Policy explains how Thenline handles information and how Apple, RevenueCat, and other platform services are involved. Thenline is provided by the individual or entity identified as the developer or provider on its Apple App Store product page ("we," "us," or "our").

## 1. Privacy at a Glance

Thenline does not require a Thenline account. Journal content is stored on your device by default and is not uploaded to a server operated by us. Thenline does not contain third-party advertising, sell personal information, or use cross-app advertising tracking. It does not infer your mood or mental state from the text you write.

Information is handled by Apple or another service only when necessary for a feature you choose to use, such as an App Store purchase, iCloud backup, Apple Health, location, or sharing.

## 2. Information You Create or Import

Thenline may process the following information on your device:

- Profile information you choose to add, including a display name, handle, and avatar.
- Journal entries, replies, quotes, topics, saved entries, tasks, drafts, and timestamps.
- Mood labels and place names you deliberately add to an entry.
- Photos, videos, voice notes, and other media you attach.
- Files you import into Thenline.

This information is stored in the app database and protected app directories. Selected media, recordings, and avatars may be copied from temporary system locations into Thenline's app storage so the operating system does not remove them as temporary files. We cannot view this local content through our own servers because Thenline does not operate a content server for journal entries.

## 3. Camera, Photos, and Microphone

Thenline requests access to the camera, photo library, or microphone only when you choose to take a photo or video, select media, save a generated poster, or record a voice note.

Selected media and recordings are used for the entry you specify and are stored in the app directory. A sharing poster is written to your photo library or passed to the system share sheet only after you choose to save or share it. Thenline does not record audio, take photos, or capture video in the background, and it does not upload this content to a server operated by us.

## 4. Location Information

Thenline requests When In Use location permission only when you choose to add a place to an entry. It obtains your current location once and uses system location and geocoding services to convert the coordinates into a readable place name.

Thenline stores the resulting place name with the entry. It does not store a continuous location history, track your location in the background, or use location for advertising or profiling. You may revoke location access in system settings.

## 5. Apple Health and State of Mind

On supported Apple devices, you may authorize Thenline to read your latest State of Mind value and write a State of Mind value that you deliberately record. Apple Health access is optional and controlled through Apple's HealthKit permission interface.

HealthKit data is not sent to us, RevenueCat, advertising platforms, or data brokers. It is not sold or used for advertising, marketing, credit, insurance, employment decisions, medical diagnosis, or data mining.

Thenline does not include HealthKit records in its exports or iCloud backups. A mood label you separately save as part of a journal entry is journal content and may be included in an export or backup you choose to create. You can change Thenline's Health permissions in system settings and manage or delete HealthKit records in the Apple Health app.

## 6. App Lock, Biometrics, and Device Passcode

When you enable App Lock, Thenline invokes Apple's system authentication interface. Face ID and Touch ID templates and your device passcode are handled by the operating system and secure hardware. Thenline does not read, store, or upload this biometric or passcode information. The app receives only an authentication result, such as success, failure, cancellation, or unavailable.

## 7. Notifications and Home Screen Widgets

Daily reminders use local notifications on your device. The reminder time is stored locally and does not require a Thenline push-notification server.

For eligible Premium users, the Home Screen widget may write a device-local snapshot containing up to 50 entries with entry text, identifiers, and creation times to a protected App Group used by Thenline and its WidgetKit extension. Free users do not write entry text to this shared area. Widget information is not sent to a server operated by us. Content shown in a widget may be visible to anyone who can view your Home Screen, subject to Apple's device and widget privacy controls.

## 8. iCloud Backup

If you purchase the applicable Premium entitlement and enable iCloud backup, Thenline uploads a ZIP backup to a private iCloud container associated with your Apple ID. The backup may contain:

- Your Thenline profile.
- Entries, replies, quotes, content blocks, topics, and saved-entry information.
- Mood labels and place names stored as journal content.
- Attached photos, videos, voice notes, and avatars.

Each new backup replaces the previous Thenline backup in that container. Apple provides the transfer, storage, account security, and availability of iCloud. We do not have a separate cloud console through which we can view your private backup.

## 9. Import, Export, and Sharing

Thenline can create a complete ZIP backup and readable Markdown or Word documents. Exported files are created locally and passed to the system destination you select. Imported backup files are parsed on your device.

Exports and shared posters may contain sensitive information. After you export or share a file, its handling is controlled by you, the destination app, storage provider, and recipient. Review files before sharing them and delete temporary or exported copies when they are no longer needed.

## 10. Purchases and RevenueCat

Purchases and payment details are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your complete card number or payment-account credentials.

Thenline uses RevenueCat to retrieve products, verify and restore purchases, and provide Premium entitlements. RevenueCat may process purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, subscription status, country or region, an anonymous App User ID, and limited device or operating-system information needed to provide the purchase service. Thenline disables RevenueCat's automatic device advertising-identifier collection, does not provide RevenueCat with your journal content, and does not use RevenueCat for advertising tracking. Production builds do not enable RevenueCat diagnostic data collection through the SDK.

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information under their own privacy policies:

- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

## 11. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only to:

- Provide the journaling, search, analysis, backup, export, widget, and other features you request.
- Deliver and restore purchases and Premium entitlements.
- Protect app functionality and security.
- Troubleshoot failures.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our agreements.
- Act on permissions or choices you make.

Thenline does not use journal text to train advertising models, personalize advertisements, or sell information to third parties.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, optional permission-based features rely on your consent; local processing is necessary to provide the features you request; purchase processing is necessary to perform our contract with you; and limited security or compliance processing may rely on legal obligations or legitimate interests.

## 12. Disclosure and International Processing

Information may be handled by Apple services, including the App Store, iCloud, HealthKit, location and geocoding services, local notifications, WidgetKit, and the system share sheet, as well as by RevenueCat for purchases. These providers may process necessary information in countries or regions other than your own under their policies, contractual commitments, and applicable safeguards.

We do not disclose journal content to third parties except when required to provide a feature you deliberately use, when you choose to share or export content, or when disclosure is required by law. Any third-party provider that processes information on our behalf is expected to protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable law.

## 13. Retention and Deletion

- Local content remains on your device until you delete it in Thenline, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
- Drafts remain only on the device unless you delete them or remove the app.
- An iCloud backup remains in your private iCloud container until it is replaced by a newer backup or deleted using Apple's iCloud management tools.
- Widget snapshots are replaced as content, entitlement status, or app state changes and are removed with the relevant app data or app installation.
- You are responsible for exported files and copies shared with other apps or people.
- Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records as required for payment processing, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and service operation.

Because Thenline has no account system or central journal-content server, we generally cannot remotely locate, access, correct, or delete content stored only on your device or in your private iCloud container.

## 14. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can:

- Delete entries and other content in Thenline.
- Revoke camera, photo, microphone, location, notification, biometric, Apple Health, or iCloud permissions in system settings.
- Manage or delete HealthKit data in the Apple Health app.
- Manage iCloud data and App Store subscriptions through Apple.
- Delete exported files from the locations where you saved them.
- Uninstall Thenline to remove its local app data, subject to device backups and data retained separately in iCloud.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. For information held only on your device or in your private iCloud, use the controls provided by Thenline, your device, and Apple. For purchase information held by Apple or RevenueCat, contact the relevant provider.

To submit a privacy question or request relating to information controlled by us, contact **zxyhdream@gmail.com**. We may need to verify the request before responding. We cannot retrieve journal content that we do not possess.

## 15. Security

Thenline uses the iOS app sandbox, protected app directories, system permissions, optional system authentication, and Apple-provided iCloud security mechanisms to reduce risk. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You should use a device passcode, protect your Apple ID, keep appropriate backups, and safeguard exported files.

## 16. Children's Privacy

Thenline is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. It does not require children to create an account or submit personal information to us. A person below the age at which they may independently consent to digital services in their jurisdiction should use Thenline only with the consent and guidance of a parent or legal guardian. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been improperly provided to us.

We may update this Privacy Policy when Thenline's features, applicable law, or third-party services change. We will revise the date at the top of the Policy and provide additional notice or request consent when required by law.
