**Last updated on November 23, 2026**

This policy applies to **pdfSign**, our PDF signing app. You can use the app without creating an account.

## 1. How we handle your information

- **Documents and photos.** The app processes the PDFs, photos, and scans you choose, along with information you enter into documents, so you can preview, edit, sign, and save them. Documents saved in the app stay on your device. The app does not automatically upload this content to the developer.
- **Signatures, initials, and stamps.** The app uses the signatures, initials, and stamps you draw, create, or import so you can add them to documents. Any you save in the app stay on your device for future use.
- **Certificate signing.** If you choose to sign with a certificate, the app uses the certificate file you select and the password you enter on your device for that signing operation only. The app does not retain your certificate file or password. A signed PDF may contain information from the signer’s certificate that recipients can view.
- **Support correspondence.** If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you and handle your support or privacy request.

We do not sell your personal information or use your documents, photos, or signatures for advertising.

## 2. Device permissions

The camera is used for scanning only when you start a scan. The app reads only the files and photos you select. Your device may request permission when you save document pages to your photo library. You can manage or revoke the app’s permissions in your device settings. The associated features may become unavailable if you revoke permission.

## 3. Sharing, backups, and third-party services

- **Exporting, sharing, and printing.** When you export, share, or print a document, the destination, app, recipient, or print service you select receives the relevant content. You can also choose to open links from documents in your browser. External services handle information under their own privacy policies.
- **Device backups.** If you enable device backups, your operating system may back up app data according to your backup settings. You can manage those backups in your device settings.
- **Document scanning on Android.** Google Play services provides the scanner and processes scans on your device. Google may collect device information, identifiers, app information, and usage and diagnostic data related to scanning to maintain and improve the service. The contents of your scans are not sent to Google as part of this data. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).
- **App ratings and reviews.** If you submit a rating or review, the relevant app store processes it and may display it publicly under its own rules.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Documents, signatures, initials, and stamps saved in the app stay on your device. You can delete them in the app at any time. Deleting a saved signature, initials, or stamp does not remove it from documents you have already signed. Copies you have exported, shared, or backed up are managed separately at their respective destinations.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request, follow up with you, or meet our legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided through support. We handle these requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 5. Policy updates and contact

We post the updated policy on this page and revise the date at the top when it changes.

If you have questions about this policy or want to submit a privacy request, go to **Settings → Contact Us** in the app or email **zxyhdream@gmail.com**.
