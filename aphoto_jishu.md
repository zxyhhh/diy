Welcome to Aphoto+. For help with photo editing, AI creation, saving creations or subscriptions, contact [zxyhdream@gmail.com](mailto:zxyhdream@gmail.com). Support is available in Chinese and English.

### 1. Getting started

- **Photo editing:** Choose a photo, then use filters, adjustments, crop, rotation and beauty tools supported by your device. Check your edits with undo, redo and before/after comparison. Some features may be unavailable depending on your device or photo.
- **Saving and exporting:** Saving to the app library and exporting to your device photo library are separate actions. To see a finished image in your device photo library, export it and confirm success. The app does not overwrite the original photo in your device photo library.
- **AI creation:** Choose a photo and an effect or template, then submit. AI creation requires internet access and an active Pro membership.
- **Language:** Choose Simplified Chinese, English or the system language in Settings.

### 2. Problems importing or exporting photos

1. Check Aphoto+'s photo permissions in system settings. If access is limited to selected photos, make sure the intended photo is included.
2. Make sure the photo has downloaded to your device and opens normally in your device photo library. Check available storage.
3. Allow photo saving when prompted during export. If saving fails, keep your draft, free up storage and try again.
4. If a photo cannot be used, follow the on-screen instructions to adjust it or try a different photo.

If you use a camera entry point, allow camera access when prompted. Denying a permission may affect the corresponding feature; you can change permissions later in system settings.

### 3. Slow, failed or interrupted AI tasks

- Check your internet connection and membership status. Generation may take some time; follow the on-screen updates.
- If creation is interrupted, use “Resume AI task” on screen to check its progress. Leaving the screen or closing the app does not cancel a creation that has already started.
- Resuming an existing creation does not use an additional creation allowance. Use the resume option first and avoid submitting repeatedly.
- If a result is temporarily unavailable, follow the on-screen instructions or contact support. Starting a new creation may use another allowance.
- Pro currently includes up to 8 AI creations per day, resetting at 00:00 Beijing time (UTC+8). Unused allowances do not roll over. Some creations that have already started may still count if they fail or are interrupted. Contact support if you have questions about your allowance.
- View and save generated results promptly; they may no longer be available after 7 days. Creations already saved to your device are unaffected.
- AI output may distort people, details or composition. Review results before saving or sharing. You can try a clearer photo or a different effect if needed.

### 4. Subscriptions, restoring purchases and refunds

- Prices, billing periods, promotional eligibility and renewal details are shown on the purchase screen and the store confirmation screen.
- If you paid but access is missing, use the original purchasing store account, connect to the internet and select “Restore purchases” on the membership screen. Restoring does not create a new subscription charge or reset the daily AI allowance. Do not purchase again just to restore access.
- On iOS, cancel renewal in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Aphoto+ → Cancel Subscription. For an Android store version, manage subscriptions in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
- Uninstalling the app, deleting creations or emailing support does not cancel a subscription. Confirm cancellation in the relevant store.
- For Apple purchases, request a refund through [Apple Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). For Google Play purchases, check your order history and [Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/) for refund options. Eligibility and outcomes depend on the store and applicable rules.

### 5. Creations, drafts and changing devices

Creations and drafts are primarily stored on your current device. After accidentally deleting a creation, check “Recently Deleted” for a recoverable item and follow the deadline shown. After emptying the trash, the app no longer offers recovery for those entries.

Creations and drafts do not currently sync automatically to other devices. Reinstalling, clearing app data or changing devices may cause content that has not been backed up to be lost. Before troubleshooting, export important finished images to your device photo library or another backup location. Avoid uninstalling as a first step. Restoring a subscription does not restore local photos or drafts.
