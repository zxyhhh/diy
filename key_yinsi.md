Keyora is provided by fuxing_app. This policy explains how Keyora handles your information.

## Information kept on your device

Keyora does not require an account. Your saved account details, verification keys, and passwords are encrypted and stored on your device. Keyora does not upload them to the developer’s servers. Delete entries individually or reset the vault to remove all saved accounts and passwords.

When you choose to scan a QR code, the camera is used to read the code. The images are not saved or uploaded. If you unlock the app with your face or fingerprint, Keyora receives only the result. Verification codes and passwords you choose to copy are placed on your device’s clipboard.

## Browsing and search

Websites you visit may receive your IP address and information you enter. Searches submitted from the address bar are sent to Google. Ending a private browsing session clears local website data such as cookies and cache. Websites and search services handle their own records under their respective privacy policies.

## Contact us

For privacy questions or requests about your data, contact [zxyhdream@gmail.com](mailto:zxyhdream@gmail.com). We use your email address and message to handle your request. Please do not email passwords, verification codes, or verification keys. The current version and date will appear on this page when the policy is updated.
