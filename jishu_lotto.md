### 2. Getting started

**Scan a ticket:** Open the scanning screen and take or select a photo. Review the game, draw date, state or region, and every number combination before saving. Use the ticket’s draw date rather than substituting its purchase date. Recognition results can be edited; use manual entry if recognition fails.

**Enter and save manually:** Select the game, date, and region, then enter five white-ball numbers and one special-ball number. White balls must be distinct within a combination. The special ball uses a separate pool and may have the same value as a white ball. Valid ranges depend on the game and draw date.

**Look up and compare:** View the latest draws on Home or choose a game and date in past draws. Open My Tickets to view saved records and recheck results when data becomes available. Missing draw data or a failed lookup does not mean a ticket lost.

**Language, appearance, and region:** Adjust language, theme, and default state or region under More.

### 3. Common issues

| Issue | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Camera or photo selection does not work | Check camera or photo authorization in system settings. Try another image or manual entry if needed. |
| Recognition fails or numbers are incorrect | Place the ticket flat in good lighting. Avoid glare, blur, obstruction, and steep angles. Keep the game, date, and complete number rows visible. Retake the photo and review every field before saving. |
| Image is too large or unsupported | Crop or reduce the image, or select a common JPEG / PNG image, while keeping ticket details legible. |
| No draw results appear | Check the game and draw date. There may be no draw for that date, or data may not yet be updated. Retry later and confirm through official channels. |
| Network timeout or service unavailable | Check connectivity, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and retry later. If the issue persists, email the error message and time of occurrence. |
| Too many requests | Pause repeated actions and try again later. Avoid rapid refreshing. |
| Reminder does not appear | Confirm a reminder was set, notifications are allowed, and the draw time is still valid. Check Do Not Disturb, Focus, and battery restrictions. Reminders are generally scheduled one hour before the draw; notification delivery may be delayed. |
| Membership or products do not load | Check your store sign-in and connection and update the app. Products may be unavailable on some platforms, regions, or versions. Repeated purchases are not a troubleshooting step. |
| Purchase completed but access is locked | Try Restore Purchases on the membership page using the original purchasing store account. Pending payments require store approval. If access remains unavailable, contact support with necessary order information. |
| Sharing is unavailable | Try updating the app or using another sharing option. Contact support if the issue persists. |

### 4. Subscriptions, restoration, and refunds

Where membership is offered, prices, billing periods, trial eligibility, and benefits are shown on the purchase screen and confirmed by the store. Restore Purchases restores verifiable active subscription access; it does not recover deleted local tickets. Access does not necessarily transfer automatically between different stores.

Manage renewal, cancel subscriptions, or request refunds through the App Store or Google Play account used for purchase. Deleting the app, clearing data, or deleting tickets does not cancel a subscription. Refund approval follows the purchasing store’s rules.

### 5. Local data and privacy

Ticket records and settings are primarily stored on the current device. Keep a copy of any records you need before changing devices. Deleting a ticket cannot be undone within the app. Clearing storage, deleting the app, or changing devices may cause record loss. Keep any information you need and your original paper tickets before taking these steps; restoration through system backups depends on device settings.

Scanning uploads your selected image for recognition. Redact unrelated information before sending support screenshots. See the [LottoKnow Privacy Policy](yinsi.md) for information handling and privacy requests.

### 6. Results and support scope

LottoKnow provides number records and reference comparisons. It does not sell tickets, accept bets, or redeem prizes. Recognition, draw data, number matching, and prize estimates may contain errors or delays. Keep your original ticket and rely on official results, ticket details, and local claim rules. Number suggestions and historical frequencies do not guarantee wins or improve winning odds.

Contact the relevant lottery operator for ticket validity, prize eligibility, claim deadlines, or official prize amounts. Contact the support email on this page for app operation, recognition, lookups, subscriptions, or technical issues.
