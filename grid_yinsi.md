This policy applies to Critter Grid. It explains how the game developer handles your information and how you can manage it. You can play without creating a Critter Grid account.

## Game data

Game progress, challenge history, your in-game profile, and settings are stored on your device to save your progress and preferences. You can delete local data by uninstalling the game or by clearing its data in your device settings where supported. Local progress may be lost if you clear app data, uninstall the game, or switch devices.

## Advertising and privacy choices

When ads are enabled, Critter Grid shows ads through third-party advertising services. These providers may collect and process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the game and ads, and app performance and diagnostic information. IP addresses can be used to estimate general location. They may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where permitted by your choices and applicable rules.

When ad privacy options are available in the game’s Settings, you can use them to review or change your choices. You can also manage your advertising identifier through your device’s ad privacy controls, where available.

## Purchases and subscriptions

The app store you purchase through handles payments and payment method information. We use a subscription service to verify purchases, check subscription status, and restore membership benefits. The service may process an identifier linked to your purchases, the items you bought, your transaction history, and your subscription status. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through the app store where you purchased them.

## Email feedback

The game’s feedback feature opens your email app. If you send us a message, we receive your email address, the message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to feedback, answer questions, and troubleshoot issues.

## Security, retention, and deletion

Data collected by our advertising and subscription services is encrypted in transit. We retain feedback emails for as long as needed to respond, provide support, and resolve related disputes. Purchase and subscription records are retained as needed to verify transactions, provide or restore benefits, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Advertising providers manage and retain advertising data under their privacy policies. Uninstalling the game removes local game data. Service providers handle the data they hold under their respective retention policies.

You can use the email address below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold or can manage. We may ask for information needed to verify your request and locate the relevant record, such as a related order number. After verification, we will handle the request in accordance with applicable law and delete relevant records when there is no further need to retain them. For data managed by an app store or another service provider, you can also use the privacy tools that provider offers.
