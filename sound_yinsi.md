This policy covers the Android and iOS versions of Noise Log: Sound Meter. It explains how the app handles information when you measure sound, save results, or use Premium features. You can use Noise Log without creating an app account.

## Sound measurements and hearing checks

When you grant microphone access and start a measurement, the app analyzes sound on your device to show sound levels, frequency spectra, and noise exposure estimates. The app does not save recordings or upload raw audio. Measurement times and sound level results are stored on your device so you can review your history and reports.

Hearing check results also stay on your device. If you choose to enter your age, it may be saved with the results. The app does not upload hearing check records.

## Location and maps

Location tagging is optional. You can tag a measurement manually or grant location permission and turn on automatic tagging. The app stores the location and a place name, when available, with the measurement on your device.

When you look up a place name or open the sound map, third-party map and location services may use your location or the map area you are viewing to display place names and maps. You can turn off automatic tagging or revoke location access in your device settings. Existing location tags remain with your saved measurements.

## Purchases and Premium access

The app store handles Android purchases. The app and its purchase verification service use purchase records to confirm Premium access and restore purchases. You can manage orders and subscriptions in the app store.

## Retention, sharing, and deletion

Measurement records, hearing check results, and app preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the app's data or delete the app. When you choose to export or share a file, the app or recipient you select can receive its contents. Exported copies and backups managed by your device need to be removed separately.

Purchase records are kept as needed to verify and restore purchases, handle refunds, and meet applicable obligations.
