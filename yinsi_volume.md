You can play and edit audio and video in Volume Booster without creating an account. This policy explains where your files are stored and what information we use to provide subscriptions or respond when you contact us.

## Your audio and video

When you choose a file or share one to Volume Booster from another app, Volume Booster opens and processes it on your device. Volume adjustments, bass boosts, equalizer changes, and exports all happen on your device. Your files stay on your device while you edit.

The app keeps imported copies, recent projects, and your language preference on your device so you can pick up where you left off. You can remove projects in the app. To delete a copy you exported elsewhere, delete it from the location where you saved it. If you choose to share a file with another app, that app handles the file under its own privacy policy.

## Optional ads on Android

After your free edits run out, you can choose to watch an ad to unlock your current edit. The advertising provider may use device identifiers, your internet address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Your audio and video are never shared with the advertising provider. Where available, you can change your ad privacy choices in Settings.

## Android subscriptions

When you open the Android app, even before you make a purchase, we and our subscription provider use your in-app user ID, basic device information, and country or region to check your membership and show available plans.

If you subscribe or restore a purchase, we and our subscription provider use your plan, purchase history, and subscription status to check trial eligibility, activate or restore your membership, and update it when you renew or cancel.

Google Play handles payments. We cannot see your full payment card number. To stop automatic renewal, cancel your subscription in Google Play’s subscription settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription.

## Contact and privacy requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. We keep support and subscription records only as long as needed to handle your request, provide your membership, or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, please use the developer contact details on the Volume Booster app store listing. We will handle your request in accordance with applicable law.
